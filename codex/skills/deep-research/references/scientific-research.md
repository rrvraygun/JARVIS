# Scientific research route

Define population/system, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcome, and time.
Check study design, sample size, measurement validity, controls, statistical and
practical significance, missing data, preregistration, replication, corrections,
retractions, funding, and generalizability. Distinguish preprints from peer review
and observational association from causal evidence. Prefer systematic reviews
only when their inclusion criteria and underlying evidence match the question.
